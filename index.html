Chilli Stars

AI + AR city exploration — AR navigation, contextual AI chat, and real-time crowd data in one app. Launching in Singapore.

Singapore · MVP live

The problem

Exploring a city as a tourist today means piecing together fragmented sources — maps, review sites, blogs, social media — with zero real-time visibility into crowding or queues, and zero cultural context delivered at the moment it actually matters: standing in front of the place itself.

Three pillars

Curated Trails

Hand-picked walking routes through Singapore's neighborhoods, hawker centres, and hidden corners.

AI Chat

Contextual answers grounded in real data about the exact spot you're standing at, powered by Gemini.

AR Navigation

Anchor-based augmented reality guidance to points of interest, right through your camera.

Who it's for

Travelers who already use travel apps and want more than a pin on a map — a curated, technology-enhanced way to experience a city, starting in Singapore, with Tokyo and Seoul next.

Status

Working MVP built solo with AI-assisted development. Flutter app, Supabase backend, automated test suite, CI/CD. Bootstrapped, zero external capital to date.
