Privacy Policy

Draft status: This document describes the actual current data practices of the app as of August 2026. It has not yet been reviewed by a Singapore-qualified lawyer. It will be updated and formally reviewed before wider public release beyond closed testing.

1. Who we are

Chilli Stars ("we", "the App") is currently operated by an individual developer as a pre-incorporation project based in Singapore's market. Data Protection Officer contact: hello@chillistars.com.

2. What we collect

  • GPS location: when you self-report a queue/crowd status at a Spot, and passively via a presence ping while the app is open near a Spot. Used only to validate and display real-time crowd/queue information.
  • Anonymous account ID: generated via Supabase anonymous authentication. No email or password is collected or required.
  • Visit history: which Spots and Trails you have viewed or visited, used to personalise recommendations.
  • AI chat messages: text you send to the in-app AI assistant is processed by Google Gemini (a third-party AI provider) to generate a response. Chat is limited to 10 messages/day per account.

3. Why we collect it

  • Location: to power crowdsourced queue/crowd status (a core feature of the app).
  • Anonymous ID: to prevent duplicate/abusive submissions and store your preferences without requiring personal identification.
  • Visit history: to improve Trail and Spot recommendations.
  • Chat messages: to answer your questions about Spots, food, and Trails.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google (Gemini API): chat messages are sent to Google's servers for processing. See Google's Gemini API terms.
  • Supabase: our backend/database provider, hosting anonymous account and location data.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with advertisers.

5. Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing hello@chillistars.com. Because your account is anonymous, we cannot identify you by name — deletion requests are matched to your device/anonymous ID.

6. Data retention

Presence ping data is retained for 30 days. Visit history and check-in data are retained until account deletion is requested.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

This is version 1.0, published August 2026. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated version and date.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related question or request, contact our Data Protection Officer at hello@chillistars.com.
